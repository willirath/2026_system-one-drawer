✏️ System-One Alphabet

Draws the alphabet from toy building blocks (square A×A, double square 2A×A, half square, roof with base 2A and height A), placed face to face. Triangles turn in quarter turns; squares and double squares can also sit diagonally, against a triangle's slanted face or another diagonal block. TypeSafe's Jev (via OpenRouter), a fast decision model, is the only judge.

It evolves 10 drawings at once. Each generation, Jev sees them side by side in a grid and answers a choice question: "Which panel shows a capital letter X?" Its probabilities are the fitness. For the pick, Jev also answers a noul question: "The image shows a capital letter X." That P tracks progress. The pick and the best drawing so far (highest P) carry over unchanged. The others are children of parents drawn by fitness: half by crossover (one parent's blocks on one side of a random cut, the other's on the other side), half by mutation (1-3 blocks added, or one taken away). A drawing has at most 8 blocks. A run stops at P > 0.66 or after 30 generations and shows the best drawing.

The alphabet

The best of 3 runs per capital letter and digit. Click one to replay it.

Write a word

Type up to 7 letters or digits: the word is set from the alphabet above. With your OpenRouter key (below), Evolve improves the word as a whole: 10 versions at once, each changing one letter or mixing letters from two parents. Jev picks from a grid, "Which panel shows the word …?", and its noul P on "The image shows the word …" tracks progress, up to 30 generations.

Draw a letter

Pick a letter or digit and draw it live with your own OpenRouter key. A run makes up to 60 Jev calls and costs well under one cent.

Your key and the drawings go only to openrouter.ai. Canned drawings come from run_examples.py, which runs the same loop. Explore other objects (house, rocket, flagpole) and draw anything you type. Code: github.com/willirath/2026_system-one-drawer
