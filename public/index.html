✏️ System-One Drawer

Draws an object from toy building blocks (square A×A, double square 2A×A, half square, roof with base 2A and height A), turned in quarter turns and placed flat face to flat face. Slanted faces don't attach. TypeSafe's Jev (via OpenRouter), a fast decision model, is the only judge.

It evolves 10 drawings at once. Each generation, Jev sees them side by side in a grid and answers a choice question: "Which panel shows a(n) <object>?" Its probabilities are the fitness. For the pick, Jev also answers a noul question: "The image shows a(n) <object>." That P tracks progress. The pick and the best drawing so far (highest P) carry over unchanged. The others are children of parents drawn by fitness: half by crossover (one parent's blocks on one side of a random cut, the other's on the other side), half by mutation (1-3 blocks added, or one taken away). A run stops at P > 0.66 or after 30 generations and shows the best drawing.

Draw your own

Type any object and draw it live with your own OpenRouter key. A run makes up to 60 Jev calls and costs well under one cent.

Your key and the drawings go only to openrouter.ai. Canned drawings come from run_examples.py, which runs the same loop. Code: github.com/willirath/2026_system-one-drawer
